# Task

Write a short paper (6 - 8 pages) on the topic described below.

You have been tasked to develop the software for a traditional enterprise that want to develop an online commercial presence. This new software is going to be hosted on the cloud.

There is also a set of existing enterprise software that the new software needs to interface to. You will have to decide whether to rehost/rebuild this software or leave it as is.
You can assume that the online business will initially be small and cost-constrained, but potentially will grow very rapidly, and may have to change as you gain new insights.

Some of the required services are:
• A website that allows a client to browse a catalog, order goods and return goods
• A blog that provides engaging information
• A payment service
• A customer relationship service (contact, help, complaints)
• Analytics to understand customers demand and buying behavior

Assume that only some services are going to be provided by a third party (how would you solve integration in that case?) and many will be developed in-house.
There is an existing fulfillment and inventory software already. It is hosted in-house. It has a traditional three tier structure. How do you interface to it? Would you move it to the cloud, or not?

What would a cloud-native approach for the new software conforming to industry best practices look like?
Describe the main elements of the architecture for the major components of your solution (as listed above) and the kind of services used. Explain why you are choosing a specific option for specific components, and potentially what alternatives were considered. Architecture diagrams (in the exhibits) are a plus.
Consider cloud provider guidance, reference architectures, architecture guidance. Consider the full range of compute options (virtual machines, containers, functions) and database options (SQL, NoSQL) and the serverless stacks available on clouds.

How would you ensure security, availability and performance?
How would you manage the operations and the lifecycle of your solution?
How would you ensure that you can expand your initial solution and adapt it to changing business and customer needs?
Please describe the development process and organization
